Version 13 Fresh Flight Reset July 2026

A clearer reset and a smoother First Class food setup on iPhone.

  • New Clear All option removes orders, reservations, and the loaded food menu
  • Clear Orders remains available when the same food menu should be reused
  • Visible version badge inside the app
  • Food inputs no longer trigger unwanted iPhone zoom
  • Larger, more responsive meal quantity controls
Version 12 Faster Service July 2026

Less hunting, less horizontal scrolling, and stronger offline reliability.

  • Juice & Water and Coffee & Teas moved to the front
  • Larger, scroll-free category grid
  • Coffee additions for Irish cream and hazelnut espresso vodka
  • Improved iPhone food-quantity controls and Home Screen offline launch
  • Common mixed-drink editors moved directly beneath their recipes
Version 11 First Class Food July 2026

Replace the paper meal tally with a live, flight-specific inventory.

  • Custom food menu and loaded quantities for First Class
  • Reserve food by seat with automatic remaining counts
  • Sold-out protection plus food tallies in Prepare and Deliver
Version 10 Redeye Ready July 2026

A calmer, clearer experience for overnight service.

  • Dark mode with a remembered sun/moon setting
  • Drink orders and modifiers are more prominent in Delivery
  • “Tap when delivered” guidance is intentionally quieter
Version 9 Correct Cabin View July 2026

The reversed map now matches what crew actually see while walking the aisle.

  • Forward view remains ABC · DEF
  • Reverse view displays FED · CBA
  • First Class reverse view displays FD · CA
Version 8 Delivery Confidence July 2026

  • More complete handoff details before marking a seat delivered
