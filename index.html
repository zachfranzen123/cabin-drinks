Install on your phone

Use it like an app

Load the tool once while online. Your seat maps, menu, and current orders are then available offline.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Open Cabin Drinks.
  3. Tap Safari’s Share button.
  4. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap Open Cabin Drinks.
  3. Open Chrome’s menu.
  4. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.

Version History

See how Cabin Drinks grew from a simple seat-and-drink pad into a complete offline service companion.

Version 11First Class FoodCurrent

Replace the paper meal tally with a live, flight-specific inventory.

  • Custom food menu and loaded quantities for First Class
  • Reserve food by seat with automatic remaining counts
  • Sold-out protection plus food tallies in Prepare and Deliver
Version 10Redeye ReadyJuly 2026

A calmer, clearer experience for overnight service.

  • Dark mode with a remembered sun/moon setting
  • Drink orders and modifiers are more prominent in Delivery
  • “Tap when delivered” guidance is intentionally quieter
Version 9Correct Cabin ViewJuly 2026

The reversed map now matches what crew actually see while walking the aisle.

  • Forward view remains ABC · DEF
  • Reverse view displays FED · CBA
  • First Class reverse view displays FD · CA
Version 8Delivery ConfidenceJuly 2026
  • Airplane control reverses the row sequence
  • Delivery cards show creams, sugars, and every modifier
  • More complete handoff details before marking a seat delivered
Version 7Coffee & Tea PolishJuly 2026
  • Cleaner Jasmine Green Tea label and coffee-specific modifiers
  • Dairy or oat milk with quantities
  • Sugar or sweetener with quantities
  • Clear Orders moved to the top of the main screen
Version 6Ready to ShareJuly 2026
  • New public landing page and installation guide
  • Quick-start instructions and help
  • Share, email contact, and feedback options
Version 5Mixed DrinksJuly 2026
  • Build Your Own mixed-drink workflow
  • Bloody Mary quick build
  • Lemon, lime, and grapefruit citrus packets
Version 4First ClassJuly 2026
  • First Class and Premium cabin switcher
  • Rows 1–4 with AC · DF seating
  • Repeated taps add quantities, including double spirits
Version 3Multiple DrinksJuly 2026
  • More than one drink per seat
  • Dairy and oat milk choices
  • Cream and sugar quantities
  • Existing saved orders migrated to the new format
Version 2Public & OfflineJuly 2026
  • Installable offline web app at drinks.hizach.com
  • Aircraft selection and premium exit-row layouts
  • Branded onboard beverage menu
Version 1First PrototypeJuly 2026
  • Tap a seat, then choose a drink
  • Prepare and Deliver views
  • Orders stored locally on the device
